# Getting Started with Celune

Welcome to Celune — your AI-powered development workspace. This guide walks you through everything from creating your account to running your first project.

---

## Sign Up

Celune is currently in beta. To create an account:

1. Go to [app.celune.ai/signup](https://app.celune.ai/signup).
2. Enter your email and create a password.
3. You will be asked for an **access code**. During the beta period, every new account requires one. If you do not have a code, you can join the waitlist from the signup page.
4. Once your code is accepted, your account is created and you are taken straight into onboarding.

---

## Meet Your Agent

After signing up, you will meet **RICK** — your AI teammate. Onboarding is a short conversation, not a form. RICK will ask you a few questions to understand how you work and what you are building.

### How it works

- RICK chats with you in a streaming conversation — just type naturally.
- Based on your answers, RICK builds a profile for your workspace.
- You will be asked to pick an **agent archetype** — this sets your agent's general style (e.g., focused executor, creative thinker, methodical planner).

### Personality customization

You can adjust how your agent communicates using a simple **humor slider**. This is the only personality control during onboarding — think of it as setting the tone between "all business" and "smart colleague at a coffee shop." You can fine-tune other personality settings later from the agent's settings page.

### Voice greeting

During onboarding, your agent may play a short voice greeting so you can hear what they sound like. Voice features (text-to-speech, voice cloning) are available after onboarding on supported plans.

## Connect Your Tools

After your conversation with RICK, you will reach the **Connections** step. This is where you link Celune to the tools you already use. All connections are optional — you can skip any of them and set them up later from Settings.

### IDE Setup (MCP)

Celune connects to your code editor through the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**. Supported editors:

- **Cursor**
- **VS Code** (with Copilot or compatible extension)
- **Windsurf**

During setup, Celune provides a configuration snippet you can paste into your editor's MCP settings. This lets your agent read and understand your codebase context.

### GitHub (optional)

Connect your GitHub account to let Celune:

- Access your repositories for context
- Create and review pull requests
- Track code changes

You will be guided through installing the Celune GitHub App on your account or organization. You choose which repositories to grant access to.

### AI Provider API Key

Celune uses a **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)** model for AI. You provide an API key from your preferred provider, and Celune uses it for all AI operations in your workspace.

Supported providers:

| Provider          | Key prefix |
| ----------------- | ---------- |
| **Anthropic**     | `sk-ant-`  |
| **OpenAI**        | `sk-`      |
| **Groq**          | `gsk_`     |
| **xAI**           | `xai-`     |
| **Google Gemini** | `AI`       |

Just paste your key — Celune **auto-detects the provider** from the key prefix. Your key is encrypted before storage and never exposed in the UI after saving.

## Your Workspace

Once onboarding is complete, you land in your workspace. Here is what you will find:

### Tasks

Your task board is the center of your workflow. Tasks have statuses like **inbox**, **in progress**, **in review**, and **done**. You can view them as a list or a board. Tasks support sub-tasks, dependencies, comments, and attachments.

### Projects

Projects group related tasks together. Celune supports several project types — features, system work, research, and plans. Each project tracks its own progress and has its own task list.

### Agents

Your agent roster shows all your AI teammates. You start with RICK, and you can configure additional agents as your team grows. Each agent has its own personality settings, chat interface, and optional voice.

### Memory

The memory system is your agents' long-term knowledge base. Agents remember context from your conversations and work. You can browse, search, and manage memory entries directly. Memory supports both keyword and semantic search.

### Analytics

The analytics dashboard shows how your workspace is performing — task velocity, agent activity, and cost tracking. Analytics is available on the Pro plan and above.

## First Steps

After onboarding, Celune generates a **Getting Started project** with starter tasks to help you explore the platform. These tasks walk you through common workflows:

- Creating and completing your first task
- Chatting with your agent
- Connecting your IDE
- Exploring your project board
- Reviewing your workspace settings

You do not need to complete them in order — just pick whatever looks interesting and jump in. Each task includes instructions and context to guide you.

### What to try first

1. **Open the task board** and look at your starter tasks.
2. **Chat with RICK** — go to the Agents page and start a conversation. Ask about your projects, get help with a task, or just explore what your agent can do.
3. **Connect your IDE** if you skipped it during onboarding — go to Settings and find the Integrations tab.
4. **Create a new task** — click the "New Task" button on your task board and describe something you want to work on.

## Getting Help

If you get stuck, Celune has a built-in **support chat** available from any page. Click the support icon to open it. You can ask questions, report issues, or submit feedback — all without leaving your workspace.

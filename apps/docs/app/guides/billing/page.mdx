# Billing & Plans

> Pricing and plan details described here reflect the current state and may change as the platform evolves.

## Plans Overview

Celune is open source. You can run it yourself for free, or let us run it for you on Celune Cloud.

|                | Open source         | Celune Cloud                                                      |
| -------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Price**      | Free (Apache-2.0)   | $25 per seat per month, or $20 per seat per month billed annually |
| **Hosting**    | Your infrastructure | Managed by Celune                                                 |
| **Agents**     | Unlimited           | Unlimited, and agents never count as seats                        |
| **Workspaces** | Unlimited           | Unlimited                                                         |
| **Memories**   | Unlimited           | Unlimited                                                         |
| **Model keys** | Bring your own      | Bring your own                                                    |

Celune Cloud has fair use and no hard caps. There is no free tier and no trial; billing starts when you subscribe.

Enterprise pricing is set with you. For custom terms or a dedicated contract, email [hello@celune.ai](mailto:hello@celune.ai?subject=Celune%20Enterprise).

## Seats

A seat is one active human member of your organization. Agents are not members, so they never use a seat.

- The subscription belongs to the organization owner and covers every organization that owner holds. Each active member is counted once, even if they belong to several of those organizations.
- Every subscription has at least one seat.
- When you add, remove, activate, or deactivate a member, Celune updates the seat count in Stripe. The change is prorated on your next invoice.

## BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)

Celune Cloud and every self-hosted install include BYOK support. You provide your own API keys for AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, etc.) and the platform routes requests through your keys directly. When using BYOK:

- **No AI usage charges from Celune** -- you pay the AI provider directly at their rates.
- Your keys are encrypted at rest and never logged.
- Manage keys in **Settings > Connections > Provider Keys**.

BYOK gives you full control over your AI spend and model selection.

## Usage Tracking

Celune tracks five usage metrics so you can see your spend and activity. Celune Cloud has no hard caps on them.

| Metric         | Unit         | Description                                                |
| -------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| LLM tokens     | tokens / USD | AI model usage (tracked as token count and estimated cost) |
| TTS minutes    | minutes      | Text-to-speech audio generation                            |
| API calls      | count        | External API requests made through the platform            |
| Tasks executed | count        | Tasks completed by agents                                  |
| Storage        | bytes        | Files and data stored in your workspace                    |

### Analytics Dashboard

The **Analytics** section provides:

- **Cost tracking** -- view LLM spend, TTS costs, and trends over time.
- **Agent performance** -- utilization, health, and error rates per agent.
- **Task velocity** -- completion rates and time-to-complete metrics.
- **Usage meters** -- current usage for each metric.

Usage meters are also visible in **Settings > Billing**.

## Subscribing and Managing Your Subscription

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Choose monthly or annual billing and click **Subscribe** to start a Stripe checkout session. The seat count is set from your active members.
3. To switch between monthly and annual, update your payment method, see invoices, or cancel, click **Manage Subscription**. It opens the Stripe customer portal.

Switching between monthly and annual is prorated. When you cancel, your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period.

If a payment fails, you keep access while Stripe retries the charge, and Celune shows a billing notice until the payment goes through.

## Access Codes

Access codes grant Celune Cloud at no cost. The Celune team issues them for Enterprise agreements and complimentary accounts only.

- Enter your access code during onboarding or in **Settings > Billing**.
- Codes may be time-limited or permanent, depending on the agreement.

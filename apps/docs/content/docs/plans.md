# Plans & Pricing

Celune offers three plans. The principle is to gate scale, not features — the Builder tier is generous for solo developers, and paid tiers unlock higher limits and team capabilities.

## Getting Started

1. **Builder** — [Sign up free](https://app.celune.ai/signup) and start building immediately
2. **Pro** — [Request access](https://app.celune.ai/signup?plan=pro) for more agents and advanced features
3. **Unlimited** — [Request access](https://app.celune.ai/signup?plan=unlimited) for teams with no limits

## Billing

Billing is managed through Stripe. You can:

- View and change your plan in Settings > Billing
- Access the Stripe customer portal for invoice history
- Monitor usage against your plan limits with usage meters

## Plan Aliases

For backwards compatibility, the following aliases exist:

- `build` and `free` map to `builder`
- `team` maps to `unlimited`
